\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

In this work, we investigated methods to forecast competence boundaries in visual RL
without rollouts. While classical representation distance reliably tracks degradation
under smooth continuous shifts (Visual Pursuit), it collapses under discrete combinatorial
perturbations (Procgen CoinRun). Semantic weighting partially recovers predictive correlation
by focusing on task-critical visual features. Furthermore, Grassmann manifold analysis
reveals distinct subspace geometric signatures between end-to-end trained and frozen encoders.
Finally, our test-time self-supervised adaptation (TTSS) mitigation demonstrates substantial
reductions in catastrophic failure modes under severe shifts.
