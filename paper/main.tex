\begin{abstract}
  Learned visual policies degrade when deployed under distribution shift, yet
  detecting \emph{when} a policy will fail before costly rollouts remains
  unsolved.
  We propose a family of representation-distance metrics that forecast
  competence boundaries in visual reinforcement learning environments
  without executing any test-time rollouts.
