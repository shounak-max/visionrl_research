\paragraph{Experimental Scope Statement.}
To ensure strict reproducibility and reporting discipline, we explicitly delineate the empirical scope of this paper:
\textbf{What was run:}
All core empirical findings are conducted on the Visual Pursuit environment ($64\times 64$ RGB observations) across DrQ-v2 and SVEA-style visual RL policies evaluated across 3 independent seeds over 50k training steps.
Empirical results include: (1) semantic distance weighting analysis on Procgen CoinRun transitions (Phase~1), (2) Riemannian Grassmannian subspace geodesic distance analysis across frozen, partially fine-tuned, and end-to-end policy encoders (Phase~2), and (3) test-time self-supervised adaptation (TTSS) evaluating floor-collapse rates under severe distribution shifts (Phase~3).
\textbf{What was not run and removed from the paper:}
We have strictly removed all references and claims regarding: (a) a Reacher environment, (b) the TACO policy representation baseline, (c) the D.Va differentiable dynamics baseline, and (d) full Procgen-based competence boundary forecasting.
